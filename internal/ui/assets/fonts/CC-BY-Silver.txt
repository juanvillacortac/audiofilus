Silver-thai.ttf is a subset of "Silver" by Poppy Works (http://poppy.works/,
https://poppyworks.itch.io/silver), with glyph design credited to Itou Hiro,
Yoon Design and Wolfgang Wozniak.

Changes: only the Thai block (U+0E00-U+0E7F) and the dotted circle (U+25CC)
were kept, and hinting was removed (fontTools subset), so that Audiofilus can
show Thai text without embedding the whole 3.7 MB font.
