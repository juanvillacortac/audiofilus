# Console models

The game mode's console models are third-party low-poly models under Creative Commons Attribution licenses. They are embedded in the binary.

**Changes made to every model:** at load time it is re-centred, rescaled and lit, and dark colours are lightened a little. The `.glb` files are unchanged, except `megadrive.glb`: only its colour texture is kept, reduced to 256 pixels (the normal and roughness maps were removed to keep the file small).
