Tracker

Make chiptune music with the D-pad, Game Boy style.

The tracker is being expanded. The next version brings a deeper song structure, instruments, tables, grooves and more, inspired by LSDJ and PICO-8. This page describes the tracker as it is today; the sections marked “Coming in the next version” will be filled in when it ships.

Overview

Open Games → Tracker. A song has four channels, like the Game Boy: two pulse channels (12.5, 25, 50 or 75 % duty), a triangle and noise. Music is written in patterns of 16 steps; the song is the order in which the patterns play, plus the tempo. Songs save themselves a second after each change.

While the tracker plays, your music pauses, and the visualizers and games react to the tracker instead.

Pattern and Song views

X switches between the two views.

Pattern

16 steps × 4 channels. Each step has a note (C-0 to B-7, or OFF), a volume (0 to F) and an effect.

ButtonDoes
D-padMove the cursor
APut the last value used
Hold A + ↑/↓Change by one
Hold A + ←/→Change by a lot (an octave for notes)
B+ADelete
L1 / R1Previous / next pattern (R1 on the last one creates a new one)
B+YMute the channel under the cursor
YPlay or stop the pattern, looping

Song

The tempo and the pattern order.

ButtonDoes
Hold A + ↑/↓Change which pattern plays at that position
AOpen that position's pattern; on the empty slot at the end, add a position
B+ADelete the position
YPlay the song from the chosen position, following the order

Each channel shows a live oscilloscope, cells light up as their notes play, and while the song plays the pattern view follows it.

Effects

EffectDoes
AxyArpeggio: the note, +x and +y semitones, cycled very fast
DnPulse duty: 0 = 12.5 %, 1 = 25 %, 2 = 50 %, 3 = 75 %
BxxPitch bend: 01–7F up, 81–FF down

The menu and your songs

START opens the tracker's menu:

  • Play the song from the beginning.
  • Export to the library: saves the song as a WAV in an audiofilus tracker folder inside your music folder, and the library picks it up.
  • Duplicate pattern and Clear pattern.
  • Songs: a new song, your saved songs, and four examples to start from (Pocket Pop, Crypt Crawl, Night Drive, Amen Chip). Opening an example saves nothing until you edit it.
  • Delete this song.

Song modes: Game Boy and Free
