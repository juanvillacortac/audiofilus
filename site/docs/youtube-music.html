YouTube Music

Search YouTube Music, listen without downloading, or save songs to your library. You bring your own yt-dlp.

Setting it up

YouTube Music appears only if there's a yt-dlp program next to audiofilus. audiofilus doesn't ship or download it.

  • Handhelds: get the yt-dlp_linux_aarch64 build from the yt-dlp releases and put it in the app's yt-dlp folder.
  • Windows: yt-dlp.exe in a yt-dlp folder next to audiofilus.exe, and ffmpeg.exe on your PATH to convert downloads to MP3.
  • Linux: in the yt-dlp folder, or anywhere on your PATH.

Using it

  • Search shows YouTube Music results next to your library's; filter with All, Library or YouTube. Songs you already have are checked.
  • Listen plays without saving (a “STREAMING” badge shows on the player); A on the player while streaming saves it. Download to library saves it with its cover and tags.
  • Downloads go through a queue; the Downloads screen shows their progress.

Bot checks and signed-in songs

Age-restricted songs play from another upload of the same song when there is one. If YouTube asks your network to confirm it isn't a bot, it usually passes in a few hours; or put a cookies.txt exported from your browser in the yt-dlp folder (how to export it).
