Modules

Tracker modules and console music from four online archives: browse, search, listen, save to your library or open in the tracker.

The archives

Open Modules from the screens bar (SELECT). Each archive is a tab on the left:

ArchiveWhat's thereBrowse
The Mod ArchiveTracker modules (MOD, XM, IT, S3M…)Most downloaded, new, random
ModlandThe biggest collection of modules and console music, in every formatRandom
VGMRipsConsole and arcade soundtracks (Mega Drive, Master System, arcade boards, NES, Game Boy…) as packs of VGM tracksMost downloaded, new, random
AMP (Amiga Music Preservation)Amiga modules, by composerNew

Each tab starts with what the archive offers to browse. Only what audiofilus can play is listed (SID and some Amiga-only formats aren't). None of them needs an account.

Browsing and listening

ButtonDoes
↑/↓Choose an archive (on the left) or a result
AListen: the file is downloaded and plays (a VGMRips pack plays all its tracks)
XSearch the archive you're on
STARTThe menu (below)

The START menu on a result has:

  • Listen.
  • Save to the library: copies the file to an audiofilus modules/<artist> folder in your music folder, and the library picks it up.
  • Open in the tracker (MOD and XM): imports it as a new tracker song you can edit. See Import and export.
  • More by the artist.
  • Search, and the ways to browse: Most downloaded, New and Random (the ones the archive has).

Downloads go through the Downloads queue, with their progress, and are kept in data/cache/modules: a file you already listened to plays straight away the next time.

In Search

The Library's Search has a Modules filter next to All, Library and YouTube (L1/R1). It searches the four archives at once; All includes them too, after your library. A on a module listens to it and START opens the same menu as above.

Good to know

  • Modland's search works on its full index, which is downloaded the first time (about 6 MB) and refreshed once a month. After that, searching Modland is instant and works offline; listening still needs the network.
  • The Mod Archive matches whole words of the file name or title.
  • VGMRips returns up to 20 packs per search. On the handheld and Linux packages every pack plays (they include libvgm, which emulates the arcade and other chips); on Windows and iPhone only Mega Drive/Genesis and Master System packs are shown (see the FAQ).
  • The archives are run by volunteers: audiofilus spaces its requests and identifies itself, so please don't hammer them.
