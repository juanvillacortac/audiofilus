audiofilus docs

Everything about using audiofilus: installing it, finding your way around, and what each screen and setting does.

audiofilus is a music player for Linux handhelds (Knulli, muOS, ROCKNIX, ArkOS and dArkOS), Linux and Windows desktops, and iPhone and iPad. The music lives in a background service, so it keeps playing when you close the player or launch a game.

New here? Start with Getting started, then hold FN on any screen to see its buttons.

Topics

  • Getting startedInstall audiofilus on your device, open it, and point it at your music.
  • LibraryBrowse, search and organize your music, and see what you've been listening to.
  • PlaybackNow Playing, the queue, the sleep timer, and how the music keeps going in the background.
  • SoundReplayGain, the equalizer, crossfade and the output rate. The service applies them, so they work with the player closed.
  • LyricsSynced lyrics that tint as they're sung, from your files or found online.
  • Radio & podcastsInternet radio from thousands of stations or your own lists, and podcasts with downloads and resume.
  • ModulesTracker modules and console music from The Mod Archive, Modland, VGMRips and AMP: listen, save or open in the tracker.
  • ServersUse a music server as another library, with streaming, caching, favorites and play counts.
  • YouTube MusicSearch YouTube Music, listen without downloading, or save songs to your library. You bring your own yt-dlp.
  • ScrobblingCount what you listen to on Last.fm and ListenBrainz.
  • ControlsButtons on handhelds, the keyboard on the desktop, and touch and mouse.
  • Visualizers & gamesEight visualizers and eight games that follow the beat of whatever is playing.
  • TrackerMake chiptune with the D-pad, the LSDJ way.
  • SettingsEvery tile in Settings, section by section.
  • Handheld systemsWhat audiofilus does differently on Knulli, muOS, ROCKNIX, ArkOS and dArkOS.
