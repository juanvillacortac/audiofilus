Settings

Every tile in Settings, section by section.

Settings are tiles: a switch changes with A; a choice opens with A, then ←/→ pick (it applies at once) and A or B close it. They're saved in audiofilus.json.

Library

  • Folder: your music folder. Rescan: find new songs. Servers: Subsonic, Navidrome, Jellyfin. Radio lists and MilkDrop presets: the folders for your own station lists and MilkDrop presets, picked with the same folder browser (by default, radio and milkdrop next to the app). Not on iPhone.

Playback

  • Shuffle, Repeat (Off, All, One), Volume (0 to 200 %), Crossfade (Off to 12 s).
  • Alarm: see Alarm.

Sound

  • ReplayGain, Equalizer (graphic or parametric), Output rate, Resampling (Auto, Battery, Quality): see Sound.
  • Crossfeed (Off, Light, Medium, Strong): see Crossfeed.

Online

  • Scrobbling: Last.fm and ListenBrainz. Lyrics from the web: look lyrics up on LRCLIB.
  • Share library (DLNA) and Receive music (DLNA): see Network. Off by default.

Screen

  • Sleep after: Never, 15 s, 30 s, 1, 2 or 5 minutes without touching anything. While music plays it only counts on Now Playing.
  • Sleep mode: Backlight turns the backlight off; OLED (black) just paints the screen black, which on an OLED screen means the pixels are off.
  • 24 h clock.
  • Color: Album (each album's colors, the default) or a fixed color to match your console: Red, Orange, Yellow, Green, Ice blue, Blue, Purple, Pink, Pearl pink, White, Platinum, Graphite, Black.

Sleep settings only show on handhelds.

System

  • Language: English, Español, Português, Français, Italiano, Deutsch, Polski, Türkçe, Русский, Indonesia, 日本語, 中文, 한국어.
  • Music sounds and Interface sounds: Off, Low, Medium or High, for the chiptune sounds of music actions and of menus.
  • Swap A/B and X/Y.

About

  • Update: shows the version. Once a day the app checks the latest release and tells you when there is a new one; press A here to install it. It downloads the package for your install (PortMaster, muOS, Linux or Windows), checks it, swaps the files and restarts, keeping your settings and data. From a terminal: audiofilus update.
  • GitHub and Buy me a coffee show a QR code.

Exit

  • Keep playing on close: whether the music keeps going when the player closes without Quit. On by default on the desktop, off on handhelds.
  • Go to background: close the player, keep the music. Quit: stop everything.

On iPhone, Output rate, Keep playing on close and Go to background don't show: the music already keeps going when you leave the app.
