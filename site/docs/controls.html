Controls

Buttons on handhelds, the keyboard on the desktop, and touch and mouse.

Hold FN (F1) on any screen to see its buttons. These work everywhere:

ButtonKeyboardDoes
SELECTTabScreens bar; twice, the previous screen
STARTMThe screen's More menu; twice quickly, sleep
YSpacePause / resume
L1 / R1Q / EPrevious / next song
L2 / R2, / .Rewind / forward 10 s
AEnterAccept / open
BEsc, BackspaceBack
XSShuffle
FNF1Hold for the controls guide

In games, START pauses instead. The left analog stick works like the D-pad everywhere. Settings → Swap A/B swaps accept and back.

With the player closed

See Controls from inside a game (FN three times) and Sleep gestures.

Touch and mouse

  • Tap rows, covers, tabs, menu tiles, Settings tiles, the player's buttons and the progress bar. Tapping a row opens it.
  • Lists scroll under your finger with momentum; the mouse wheel scrolls them too.
  • A long press (half a second) is START; right-click is B; middle-click pauses.
  • The button hints at the bottom can be tapped; with touch they become real named buttons.
  • In portrait, the library, Radio and Podcasts tabs open from a button as a drop-down.
  • Games have their own touch controls (see Visualizers and games).
